Home Buyer's Guide & Tools

It takes more than a trained real estate agent to understand the needs and concerns of buyer clients. It takes a team who has been there, someone who has the same concerns when they're home shopping. There's plenty to do. Check out my home buyer services below...

Home Search Assistance

I help my buyers understand the many fields of information in an MLS, Multiple Listing Service search. I guide them in making the most of searches in an MLS with thousands of listings.

Automated Search Reports

Once I have an idea of your criteria for a home selection, I can set up automated search alert emails to keep you informed of new listings and price reductions of qualified properties.

Financing Assistance

As tough as it is these days to get a mortgage, I know the best sources in the market and will help you to contact mortgage professionals who have been very helpful to my past clients.

Price Negotiations

Buyers shouldn't walk into a deal unarmed. I arm you with market data to help you to understand the buyer and seller sides and formulate an offer price negotiation strategy.

Inspections & Repair Negotiations

Price isn't the only negotiation, as there will be property inspections and you may need to enter into more negotiating around property condition and repairs. I am an expert at this.

Contract to Closing

The myriad of tasks, documents and delivery deadlines involved in a real estate transaction are all coordinated by me and our closing assistance staff. I am here to help.

Home Seller's Guide & Tools

Sellers have enough to worry about in readying their home for market and planning their move to a new home. I will take over a huge number of tasks and details to make sure that your real estate property is properly evaluated, priced, marketed, negotiated and carried to closing.

A Marketing Plan that Works

We have perfected a marketing plan for every type of property in the market. Some things work better in some areas and price ranges than others. I'll craft a unique plan for your property.

Thorough & Objective Evaluation

I consider it my obligation to help you thoroughly and objectively evaluate your home's condition and features to properly assess how it competes in the current real estate market.

CMA Market Analysis

I help my clients to value their property through thorough CMA, Comparative Market Analysis reports using current listing price data and past sold comparable property sales.

Search Assistance

It's important that I help you to search the MLS® so you can learn about currently listed similar homes in your area. Only by knowing the competition you can set a competitive price.

Regular Market Updates

Markets don't just change monthly, they change daily in many instances. As new listings come on the market and others sell, I provide you with regular market updates to keep you in the loop.

Price Negotiations

I will be with you every step of the way in the contract price negotiations and will work to get you the top price possible with the fewest concessions.

Inspections & Repairs

The negotiations rarely end with price. When buyers request corrective action involving expense to the seller, I'm here to work with you every step of the way!

Contract to Closing Process

There are more than 50 tasks involved in taking a seller side transaction from contract to closing. I'm on top of all of them to make sure you leave with a check.

Here Is How I Could Help

RESIDENTIAL SINGLE FAMILY

My residential single family services connect buyers with sellers every day, and I do it with professionalism and a total dedication to my clients. This property type is the majority of my market transactions, but by far not the only one in which we have expertise.

CONDOMINIUMS

While condominiums are residential, they're a very different market focus, and I am an expert in evaluating condos and helping buyers and sellers to cope with the special financing and homeowner concerns for this property type.

MULTI-FAMILY
